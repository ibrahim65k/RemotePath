ABOUT REMOTEPATH

online work.

Work is changing.
Finding your way shouldn’t feel impossible.

RemotePath is an independent educational reference for discovering online professions and understanding the paths, skills, tools, and platforms associated with them.

We bring a broad landscape into focus, with practical context for people in Egypt exploring global opportunities. RemotePath helps you learn what a field involves and decide what you want to explore next.

It is a guide for discovery — not a job marketplace, recruitment service, or course platform.
